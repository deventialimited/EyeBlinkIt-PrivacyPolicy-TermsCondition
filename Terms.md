## What EyeBlinkIt is

EyeBlinkIt is a wellness tool that reminds you to take short screen breaks based on the 20-20-20 rule recommended by the American Optometric Association, and offers guided relaxation exercises for your eyes.

- The exercises are general relaxation techniques. Stop if you feel pain, discomfort or dizziness.
- If you have eye pain, sudden vision changes, or have had recent eye surgery or injury, talk to an eye doctor before doing the exercises.
